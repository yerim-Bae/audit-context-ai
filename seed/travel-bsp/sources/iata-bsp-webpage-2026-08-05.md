## 본문 (수집된 범위)

**Billing and Settlement Plan (BSP)**

Simplify financial relations between airlines and accredited-agents with a trusted framework and intelligent solutions

Global air travel is a complex business, with thousands of airlines and tens of thousands of travel agents transacting every second. In this high-volume, high-risk environment, a trusted financial system is essential. The IATA Billing and Settlement Plan (BSP) provides a standardized global framework that simplifies sales reporting, remittance, and settlement between airlines and IATA-accredited travel agents.

For over 50 years, BSP has been the trusted backbone of airline distribution, helping airlines and accredited travel agents simplify reporting, remittance, and settlement while enhancing financial control, transparency, cash flow, and risk management through BSPlink, and supporting modern retailing and new payment methods.

**Manage your network from a single place**

IATA's BSP spans 207+ countries and territories, and currently serves over 59,000 of the world's largest travel brands, including over 400 airlines worldwide, with an on-time settlement rate of 99.999%. In 2023, it processed over $240 billion.

With the BSP, you can:

- Access the most cost-effective settlement platform with a $0 fee for processing and settling transactions in 2024 to IATA Members.
- Manage selling, reporting and payments with your BSP partners in a single process – agents issue one sales report and remit one amount to a central point; airlines receive one settlement covering all agents.
- Monitor centrally your credit card consents and setup and access to a central production and distribution of card remittance files.
- Centralize all your payment and revenue-related information and processes.
- Access consolidated reporting and features through BSPlink, our web-based software.

**Meet your objectives**

- Join a BSP – Participation in a BSP is open to:
  - All airlines (IATA members and non-members) serving the country or area concerned,
  - All IATA-accredited travel agents in the BSP country of operation.
  - General Sales Agents (GSA) and Airport Handling Agents (AHAs), which may be nominated to participate by the airline they represent and subject to the airline entering into a standard agreement.
- To obtain the local participation criteria and conditions, please contact the customer service for your region.
